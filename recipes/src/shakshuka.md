# Shakshuka

Serves: 2
Time: 30 min

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 4 eggs
- salt, to taste
- parsley, to garnish

## Steps
1. Warm the oil in a lidded pan and soften the onion and pepper. [8 min]
2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook until fragrant. [1 min]
3. Pour in the tomatoes, season, and simmer until thickened. [10 min]
4. Make four wells and crack in the eggs; cover and cook until the whites set. [6 min]
5. Scatter with parsley and serve from the pan.
