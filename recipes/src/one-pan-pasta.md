# One-pan tomato pasta

Serves: 2
Time: 20 min

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti
- 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 700 ml water
- salt, to taste
- basil, to garnish
- 30 g parmesan, grated

## Steps
1. Lay everything except the basil and parmesan in a wide pan; season well.
2. Bring to a boil and cook, turning the pasta with tongs as the water reduces. [9 min]
3. Keep tossing until the liquid becomes a glossy sauce that coats the strands. [3 min]
4. Serve with basil and parmesan.
