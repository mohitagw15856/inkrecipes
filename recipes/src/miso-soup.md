# Miso soup

Serves: 4
Time: 15 min

## Ingredients
- 1 l water
- 1 piece kombu, postcard sized
- 15 g bonito flakes
- 3 tbsp miso paste
- 200 g silken tofu, cubed
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp dried wakame

## Steps
1. Steep the kombu in the water as it comes slowly to a bare simmer, then remove it. [10 min]
2. Add the bonito flakes, cut the heat, and let them settle before straining. [2 min]
3. Soak the wakame in the strained dashi and add the tofu.
4. Loosen the miso in a ladleful of broth, stir it back in, and warm gently without boiling. [2 min]
5. Serve with the spring onions.
