# Dal tadka

Serves: 4
Time: 40 min

## Ingredients
- 250 g split red lentils, rinsed
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 3 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp garam masala
- salt, to taste

## Steps
1. Simmer the lentils with the turmeric and a litre of water until soft, skimming any foam. [20 min]
2. Heat the ghee in a small pan and crackle the cumin and mustard seeds. [1 min]
3. Add the garlic and onion to the ghee and fry until golden. [6 min]
4. Stir in the tomatoes and garam masala and cook down to a jammy paste. [5 min]
5. Pour the tadka over the lentils, season with salt and simmer briefly. [3 min]
