# Kimchi fried rice (kimchi bokkeumbap)

Serves: 2
Time: 25 min

## Ingredients
- 300 g kimchi, well fermented, chopped
- 2 tbsp kimchi juice
- 400 g cooked short-grain rice, day-old
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 2 eggs
- 1 sheet gim (roasted seaweed), crumbled

## Steps
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat.
2. Fry the chopped kimchi until it softens and smells sweet. [4 min]
3. Add the gochujang and kimchi juice and stir through.
4. Add the rice, breaking clumps, and fry, pressing it into the pan so it catches a little. [5 min]
5. Season with soy sauce and sesame oil; fold in most of the spring onion.
6. Fry the eggs sunny side up in a separate pan. [3 min]
7. Serve topped with the egg, remaining spring onion and crumbled gim.
